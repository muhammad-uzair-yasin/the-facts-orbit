# The Facts Orbit — content plan

## What it is
One surprising, **verified** fact per day, acted out by the stick figure in a world built from the topic.

## Niches
- **Original (Oct 30–31):** space, history, nature, world & culture.
- **Added Oct 31 (Uzair):** human nature / psychology, science, **"what if"** scenarios (what if X happened, what would happen).
- **Added Oct 31 from a competitor screenshot:** psychology/self-improvement listicles ("5 weird things your brain does", "5 signs...") with **Part 2** sequels — this stick-figure niche gets 1K–3K views per Short on small channels. Keep our own character/style; only the content format is borrowed.

## Why
- Facts were split out of the AI channels so those stay purely AI (Oct 31).
- Hook-first + real knowledge (rules.md #1–2): every fact must survive a source check; numbers exact.
- Mixing niches across the week keeps the feed varied; "what if" and psychology are the strongest hooks.

## Script shape (~1 min)
Hook = the fact/question itself → the setup → the twist/number acted out → what it means → "Subscribe for a new fact every single day."
**Cover frame:** first frame = one big yellow outlined keyword (MOON, BRAIN, SMOKE…) so the Shorts grid is clickable.
