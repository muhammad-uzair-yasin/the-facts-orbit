# Why Sunsets on Mars Are Blue

(As recorded by Uzair in ElevenLabs.)

Why is the sunset on Mars...

blue?

In 2015,

NASA's rover filmed a sunset on Mars...

and it was blue.

On Earth, air scatters blue light across the whole sky...

so sunsets burn red.

Mars flips it.

Its air is thin...

but packed with dust as fine as smoke.

That dust scatters the red light away...

and lets blue pass straight through to your eyes.

So a cold blue glow wraps the sun...

in a butterscotch sky.

And that twilight can last up to two hours.

Red on Earth...

blue on Mars.

Which one would you watch?
