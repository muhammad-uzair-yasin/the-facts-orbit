How were the pyramids really built? Not by aliens... and not by slaves.

The Great Pyramid is made of more than two million stone blocks. For centuries, nobody knew how they were moved.

Then, in 2013, archaeologists found the oldest written papyrus ever discovered... a logbook. It belonged to a man named Merer, who led about forty workers. His team carried limestone to Giza... by boat, along the Nile. About thirty giant blocks per trip.

On land, they used wooden sledges. An ancient Egyptian painting shows a man pouring water on the sand in front of one. In 2014, physicists tested it. The right amount of water... cuts the pulling force in half.

And the builders? Archaeologists found their village and their tombs, right beside the pyramids. These were paid workers... buried with honor.

No magic. Just boats, water... and brilliant engineering.
