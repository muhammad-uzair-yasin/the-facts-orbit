What if you fell into a black hole?

It depends... on the size of the black hole.

Fall into a small one, a few times the mass of our Sun, and gravity pulls much harder on your feet than on your head. You get stretched... thinner and thinner... like a noodle. Scientists actually call this spaghettification.

But a supermassive black hole, like the giant one at the center of our galaxy, is gentler at the edge. You could cross the point of no return... and feel nothing at all.

Now here's the strange part. To a friend watching from far away, you'd never actually fall in. You'd slow down... freeze at the edge... and fade into red, forever.

But for you, time runs normally. You fall straight through.

Two versions of the same story... and both are true.
