## Sources / fact check
- Light and gravity both travel at the speed of light: the Sun's light (and the loss of its gravity) would take ~8 min 20 s to reach Earth.
- Without the Sun's pull, Earth would continue in a straight line along the tangent of its orbit.
- The Moon shines only by reflected sunlight; the planets would also go dark.
- Temperatures ~0°F within a week and ~-100°F within a year (Popular Science / Live Science estimates).
- Deep hydrothermal-vent ecosystems run on chemosynthesis, not sunlight; surface ice would insulate the ocean below.
