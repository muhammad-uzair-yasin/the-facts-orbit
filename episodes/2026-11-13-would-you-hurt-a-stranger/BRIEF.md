## Sources / fact check
- Stanley Milgram, Yale University, obedience studies 1961–63; shock generator labelled 15 V to 450 V in 30 steps (Slight Shock … Danger: Severe Shock, XXX).
- The "learner" was a confederate (actor); no real shocks were delivered. Scripted protests: screams, banging on the wall, then silence.
- Experimenter prods included "The experiment requires that you continue."
- 26 of 40 participants (65%) went to 450 V in the baseline study; psychiatrists polled beforehand predicted about 0.1% (1 in 1,000) would.
- Variation with two peer "teachers" who refused: only 10% of participants continued to the maximum.
