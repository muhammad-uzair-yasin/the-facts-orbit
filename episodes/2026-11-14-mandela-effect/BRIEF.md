## Sources / fact check
- Term coined by Fiona Broome in 2009 after finding many people "remembered" Nelson Mandela dying in prison in the 1980s.
- Mandela was released in 1990, became President of South Africa in 1994 and died in 2013.
- The Empire Strikes Back line is "No, I am your father."
- Mr. Monopoly (Rich Uncle Pennybags) has no monocle; Pikachu's tail has no black tip.
- Prasad & Bainbridge (2022), Psychological Science (University of Chicago): the "visual Mandela effect" — people consistently make the same specific false memories about certain famous images.
- Reconstructive memory: recall is rebuilt each time and gaps are filled with schema-consistent details.
