## Sources / fact check
- Yawning: brain-cooling hypothesis (Andrew Gallup; cold-pack study — people with a cold pack on the forehead yawned less while watching yawns).
- Contagion stronger with kin/friends than strangers (Norscia & Palagi 2011).
- Children rarely catch yawns before ~4–5 years.
- Chimpanzees and dogs catch yawns (dogs from humans).
- Foetal yawning from ~11 weeks.
