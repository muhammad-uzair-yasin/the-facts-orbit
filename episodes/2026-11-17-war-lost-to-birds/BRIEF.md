## Sources / fact check
- Emu War: Nov–Dec 1932, Western Australia (Campion district); ~20,000 emus raiding wheat.
- Major G.P.W. Meredith (Royal Australian Artillery), two Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds.
- A gun jammed when a large flock came close; a gun was mounted on a truck but the emus outpaced it (emus run ~50 km/h).
- Meredith reported 986 kills with 9,860 rounds (~10 rounds per bird).
- Meredith's quote (paraphrased in the script): if we had a military division with the bullet-carrying capacity of these birds it would face any army in the world.
