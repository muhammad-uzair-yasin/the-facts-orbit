## Sources / fact check
- Randy Gardner 1964 (age 17, San Diego), 264.4 h (~11 days) awake, observed by William Dement.
- Vision/focus problems from day 2; hallucination (street sign taken for a person) around day 4; by the end, trouble with simple arithmetic and memory lapses mid-sentence.
- Slept ~14 h afterwards and recovered.
- Guinness stopped accepting sleep-deprivation record attempts (health risk).
- 17–19 h awake ≈ performance at 0.05% BAC (Williamson & Feyer 2000); microsleeps — brief lapses of a few seconds.
