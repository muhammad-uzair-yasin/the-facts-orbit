[am_eric:asker] Did a man really hijack a plane, jump out... and vanish?

[am_adam:host] November 24th, 1971. A quiet man in a suit buys a ticket in Portland under the name Dan Cooper.

[am_adam:host] Soon after takeoff, he hands a flight attendant a note. It says he has a bomb.

[am_eric:asker] What did he want?

[am_adam:host] Two hundred thousand dollars... and four parachutes. In Seattle, he got the money, and let every passenger go.

[am_eric:asker] Then what?

[am_adam:host] The plane took off again, heading for Mexico. Somewhere over Washington state, at night, in the rain... he opened the back stairs and jumped.

[am_eric:asker] With the money?!

[am_adam:host] With the money. In a suit and loafers. He was never seen again.

[am_eric:asker] They never found anything?

[am_adam:host] In 1980, an eight-year-old boy digging by a river found bundles of rotting twenty-dollar bills. The serial numbers matched the ransom. The rest of the money... and the man... are still missing.
