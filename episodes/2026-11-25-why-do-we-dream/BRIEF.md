## Sources / fact check
- Roughly 2 hours of dreaming per night in total, REM-heavy; most dreams are forgotten within minutes of waking.
- Wamsley & Stickgold et al. 2010 (Harvard / Beth Israel Deaconess, *Current Biology*): people who dreamed about a virtual maze during a nap improved about 10× more than others.
- Threat-simulation theory (Antti Revonsuo, 2000): dreams rehearse threats; chase and falling dreams are among the most common themes.
- People born blind dream without visual images, in sound, touch and smell (e.g. Meaidi et al. 2014, *Sleep Medicine*).
