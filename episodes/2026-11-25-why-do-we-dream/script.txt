[bf_alice:asker] Why do we dream?

[am_adam:host] Every night, you dream for around two hours in total. You just forget almost all of it within minutes of waking up.

[bf_alice:asker] Two hours?! So what's the point?

[am_adam:host] Scientists aren't fully sure. But one big idea is memory. While you sleep, your brain replays your day and decides what to keep.

[bf_alice:asker] Is there proof?

[am_adam:host] In 2010, Harvard researchers had people learn a maze, then take a nap. The ones who dreamed about the maze got about ten times better at it.

[bf_alice:asker] Whoa. So dreams are like practice?

[am_adam:host] Another idea says exactly that. Dreams are a safe place to rehearse danger. That's why so many dreams are about being chased... or falling.

[bf_alice:asker] What about people who are born blind?

[am_adam:host] They dream too. Not in pictures, but in sounds, touch, and smells.
