## Sources / fact check
- Pantheon, Rome, completed c. 126 AD (~1,900 years); still the largest unreinforced concrete dome.
- Many modern reinforced-concrete structures show cracking and deterioration within roughly 50–100 years.
- Seymour, Masic et al. 2023, *Science Advances* (MIT): lime clasts from hot mixing act as a calcium reservoir; when water enters a crack they dissolve and recrystallise as calcium carbonate, sealing it.
- Jackson et al. 2017, *American Mineralogist* (University of Utah): seawater reacting with volcanic ash in Roman marine concrete grows aluminous tobermorite and phillipsite crystals, strengthening it over time.
