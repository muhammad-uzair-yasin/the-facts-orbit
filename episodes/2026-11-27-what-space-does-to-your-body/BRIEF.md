## Sources / fact check
- Spinal elongation in microgravity up to ~3% of height (~5 cm), reversed on return and often with back pain (NASA).
- Fluid shift toward the head: "puffy face, bird legs" (NASA).
- Bone loss of ~1–1.5% per month in weight-bearing bones (hips, spine); ISS crews exercise ~2 hours a day (NASA).
- NASA Twins Study (Garrett-Bakelman et al. 2019, *Science*): Scott Kelly 340 days on the ISS, identical twin Mark on Earth; gene-expression changes and telomere lengthening in flight, most changes returned to baseline after return, some persisted.
