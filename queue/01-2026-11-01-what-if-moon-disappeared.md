## Script (record exactly)

What if the Moon disappeared tonight?

At first... you'd only notice the dark. Nights with no moonlight at all. Darker than anything humans have ever known.

But the real damage starts at the shore. The Moon pulls on our oceans — that's what makes tides. Without it, only the Sun is left pulling... and tides shrink to about a third of their size. Coastlines that depend on tides — crabs, birds, whole ecosystems — start to collapse.

Then comes the slow danger. Earth leans at about twenty-three degrees, and the Moon works like a hand on a spinning top, holding that lean steady. That steady lean is why our seasons are gentle. Take the hand away... and over time, the tilt can wander. Look at Mars — no big moon, and its tilt swings by twenty-five degrees or more.

So the Moon isn't just a light in the sky... it's our planet's stabilizer.
