# 5 Weird Things Your Brain Does Every Day (Part 1)

Status: approved script, awaiting Uzair's recording.
Cover word: **BRAIN**

## Script (record exactly)

Five weird things your brain does... every single day.

One. You can't tickle yourself. Your brain predicts your own touch... and turns it down before you even feel it.

Two. Walking through a doorway makes you forget. In 2011, researchers at Notre Dame found people forgot more right after passing through a door. Your brain files the old room away... like closing a chapter.

Three. Your phone buzzed... but it didn't. In one study, almost nine out of ten college students had felt a vibration that never happened. Your brain expects it so much... it invents one.

Four. Say any word over and over — spoon, spoon, spoon, spoon — and it suddenly sounds meaningless. It's called semantic satiation. The brain cells for that word get tired... and fire less.

Five. You're looking at your nose right now. It's in your view all day long... but your brain edits it out, because it never changes.

Your brain isn't showing you the world. It's showing you its best guess.
