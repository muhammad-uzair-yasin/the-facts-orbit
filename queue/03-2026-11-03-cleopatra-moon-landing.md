# Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing than to the Great Pyramid

Status: approved script, awaiting Uzair's recording.
Cover word: **CLEOPATRA**

## Script (record exactly)

Cleopatra lived closer in time to the Moon landing... than to the building of the Great Pyramid.

The Great Pyramid of Giza was finished around 2560 BC. Cleopatra ruled Egypt until 30 BC. That's about two and a half thousand years between them.

Think about that. The pyramids were older to Cleopatra... than Cleopatra is to us.

Now go forward. From Cleopatra to Neil Armstrong stepping onto the Moon in 1969... about two thousand years.

So on the timeline, Cleopatra sits closer to rockets and smartphones... than to the people who built the pyramids.

Ancient Egypt didn't last hundreds of years. It lasted thousands. And much of what we call "ancient"... was already ancient to the ancients.
