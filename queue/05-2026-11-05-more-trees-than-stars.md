# There are more trees on Earth than stars in our galaxy

Status: approved script, awaiting Uzair's recording.
Cover word: **TREES**

## Script (record exactly)

There are more trees on Earth... than stars in our galaxy.

The Milky Way has somewhere between one hundred and four hundred billion stars. Sounds impossible to beat.

But in 2015, a team at Yale counted Earth's trees — using satellite images and over four hundred thousand measurements on the ground. The answer: about three trillion trees.

Three trillion. That's around four hundred trees for every person alive.

But here's the twist. When humans first started farming, Earth had nearly twice as many. We've cut down almost half of all the trees on the planet. And we still lose around fifteen billion trees... every single year.

So yes — more trees than stars. For now.
