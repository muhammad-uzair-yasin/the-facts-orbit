# Nobody is looking at you as much as you think

Status: approved script, awaiting Uzair's recording.
Cover word: **SPOTLIGHT**

## Script (record exactly)

Nobody is looking at you... as much as you think.

In 2000, psychologists at Cornell had students put on a t-shirt with a giant face of Barry Manilow — a singer most of them found deeply embarrassing. Then they walked into a room full of people.

Afterward, the students guessed how many people noticed the shirt. Their guess... about half the room.

The real number? About a quarter.

They believed twice as many people noticed... as actually did.

It's called the spotlight effect. You're the center of your own world... so you assume you're the center of everyone else's too. But everyone else is busy... worrying about their own shirt.

That bad hair day. That mistake in the meeting. Almost nobody remembers.

So go ahead. Do the thing.
