# What if Earth stopped spinning?

Status: approved script, awaiting Uzair's recording.
Cover word: **STOP**

## Script (record exactly)

What if Earth suddenly stopped spinning?

If you live near the equator, you're moving at about one thousand six hundred and seventy kilometers per hour — right now. You just can't feel it, because everything moves with you. The air. The oceans. The ground.

Now stop the planet... but not the things on it.

Everything not bolted to bedrock keeps flying east at that speed. People, cars, buildings, trees. The atmosphere becomes a wind faster than any hurricane in history... scraping the surface clean.

Then the oceans move. Earth's spin makes it bulge at the middle. Without the spin, the water slides toward the poles... leaving one giant continent wrapped around the equator.

And one day would last... a whole year. Six months of daylight. Six months of night.

The good news? Earth's spin is slowing by only about two milliseconds per century. We're fine.
